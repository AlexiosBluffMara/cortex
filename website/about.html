Red Team Kitchen is a one-person research-and-software lab in Bloomington–Normal, Illinois. Everything you see on this site — the brain scans, the four narration voices, the local-first agent, the public demo URL — is built and shipped by one operator out of a desktop with an RTX 5090.

The four names you'll see

Soumit Lahiri

Operator · Founder · Sole engineer

The human running the lab. Designs the architecture, writes the code, files the LLC paperwork, and answers the email. Background in trading and software systems; current obsession is local-first AI on consumer hardware — the thesis that an RTX 5090 in Illinois can do real research-grade neuroscience demos for ~$3 a month in electricity.

Illinois State University (ISU)

Research collaboration partner

Illinois State University is a public research university in Normal, Illinois, founded in 1857 — the oldest public university in Illinois. Cardinal red #CC0000 is the brand color; Reggie Redbird is the mascot; the visual language of every page on this site is a respectful nod to ISU's published brand guidelines (without using ISU's protected wordmarks or seals). Cortex's research is conducted in association with ISU; that's what the "Cardinal & Code" tagline points to.

Town of Normal, Illinois

Where Seratonin (the GPU) lives

Normal is the smaller half of the "twin cities" Bloomington–Normal in central Illinois — population ~52,000, home to ISU, a Rivian assembly plant, and the desktop computer (call sign: Seratonin) that runs every demo on this site. When you click the live Cortex link, the request travels from your browser to a Tailscale Funnel endpoint to a single RTX 5090 sitting in a house in Normal. That's the entire production stack. No AWS region. No cloud GPU pool. Just a town and a card.
